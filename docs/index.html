ACPI v0.2

AI Compute Price Index
What is this? ACPI tracks how expensive AI compute is — like a stock index, but for renting GPUs and using AI models. It mixes three signals: GPU rental prices, AI model API prices, and data-center electricity.

How to read this page:
• Top cards = today's raw prices (just averages, in dollars/cents).
• ACPI score / correlation / PCA charts = formal statistics from the methodology paper (z-scores, weighted dot product, PCA). These activate after 7 days of data.
Layer 1 — GPU rental (50%)
How much it costs to rent the H100 chips that train and run AI models.

$/GPU/hour · avg of 4 clouds
Layer 2 — API tokens (27%)
How much you pay providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) per million tokens of text in/out.
—
$/M tokens · 2:1 input:output
Layer 3 — Power (13%)
Industrial electricity cost in datacenter-heavy U.S. states (VA, TX, OR, IA, CA).
—
¢/kWh · weighted average
Layer 4 — Market (10%)
NVIDIA stock + AI ETFs (CHAT, IRBO). Adds market sentiment volatility.
—
$ · avg close · NVDA + ETFs

ACPI composite score

A weighted z-score combining all three layers. Positive = AI compute is heating up vs the last 30 days. Negative = cooling down. Activates after 7 days of data.
